Honest version: your browser records ~15 seconds of you talking. A server transcribes it (Whisper) and Claude reads the dialect from your word choices and phrasing — دلوقتي، هسا، دابا، وايد all give you away.

That's why there's no script to read — if everyone said the same words, there'd be nothing to catch. Just talk naturally.

Your audio is analyzed and discarded — nothing is stored. Phase 2 is an acoustic model that hears the accent itself.
